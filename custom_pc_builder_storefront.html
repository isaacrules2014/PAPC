Support & Policies

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

What is your warranty policy?

We provide a 3-year labor warranty covering assembly, cable routing, and stability troubleshooting. Individual parts (GPU, CPU, Motherboard) also carry their original manufacturer warranties ranging from 3 to 10 years.

Do you include original boxes and accessories?

Yes! When you receive your PC, you will also receive a box containing all spare modular power supply cables, motherboard manuals, extra screws, and the original retail boxes for your components.

How long does assembly and testing take?

Standard builds take 3 to 5 business days from parts ordering to final stress testing and Windows 11 activation. We never ship a PC without completing 24 hours of benchmark stress tests.
